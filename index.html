🔬 Primary Data Sources & Computational Engines

This application operates entirely on the client side without servers, backend databases, proprietary tracking, or API keys:

  • PubChem (PUG REST & PUG View): Molecular records, computed 3D conformers, physical properties, and aggregated supplier GHS hazard classifications (National Center for Biotechnology Information, NLM, NIH).
  • RDKit.js MinimalLib (WASM): In-browser cheminformatics toolkit for 2D coordinate generation, clean vector SVG depictions, Wildman-Crippen logP descriptor calculation, and SMARTS substructure pattern matching (Greg Landrum et al.).
  • 3Dmol.js: WebGL-based molecular graphics engine for hardware-accelerated 3D conformer rendering (Nicholas Rego & David Koes, University of Pittsburgh).
  • NCI Chemical Identifier Resolver (CIR): Secondary identifier resolution connecting compound names to standardized InChIKeys (National Cancer Institute).
  • ChEBI & ChEMBL: Chemical Entities of Biological Interest ontology roles and bioactivity clinical trial phases (European Bioinformatics Institute, EMBL-EBI).
  • JSME Molecule Sketcher: Interactive 2D chemical structure editor (Peter Ertl & Bruno Bienfait).

⚠️ Known Scientific Limitations

  • Computed 3D Conformers: 3D coordinate structures retrieved from PubChem are computed theoretical conformers (energy-minimized using MMFF94s force fields), not experimental single-crystal X-ray diffraction determinations.
  • Compounds without 3D Records: Elemental salts (e.g., sodium chloride), elemental metals, and certain unassigned stereoisomers do not possess 3D records in PubChem. When 3D conformers are unavailable, the app automatically falls back to 2D coordinate layouts and marks them honestly.
  • LogP Methodological Differences: PubChem XLogP3-AA uses an empirical atom-additive prediction with calibration factors, whereas RDKit Crippen logP uses 68 Wildman-Crippen atomic environment fragment contributions. Differences of 0.5 to 1.5 log units are normal between these independent computational models.
  • GHS Supplier Variability: GHS hazard classifications represent aggregated notifications from chemical manufacturers under UN GHS, EU CLP, and OSHA HazCom. Supplier consensus and classification categories may vary between vendor submissions.
